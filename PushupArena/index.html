Privacy Policy · Last updated 23 June 2026

Rep Clash respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Camera

Rep Clash uses your camera only to detect your body and count pushups, in real time and entirely on your device. Camera frames are never recorded, never saved, and never sent anywhere. Nothing from your camera leaves your phone.

What we store

To show the public leaderboard, the app stores in Google Firebase the display name you choose, your total pushup count, and a random id created on your device. We do not collect your real name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. There is no account and no login.

The leaderboard

The display name you pick and your total reps are visible to other players in the ranking. Pick a nickname if you prefer to stay anonymous.

Ads and tracking

Rep Clash contains no advertising and no third party analytics or tracking.

Children

Rep Clash is a general fitness app and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Delete your data

To remove your leaderboard entry, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.
